We investigate the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models, exploring how truthfulness is represented in the internal representations of LLMs. Our findings reveal a consistent hyperplane structure that correlates with truthfulness across different model sizes and architectures. The code is available on GitHub (Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane).
